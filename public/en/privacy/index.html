Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026 · Version 1.0 · The Romanian version is the reference version; this is a translation.

In short. BIBS helps you organise your group's football games. To do that we use your name, email address, profile picture (if you add one), the teams you belong to, your attendance and the skill ratings set by team admins. We do not sell data, show ads, use trackers or track you across other apps or websites. You can delete your account at any time, right inside the app.

4. Who sees what in the app

  • Members of your teams see your name, nickname, picture, position, signups, the side you were placed in and the match history of that team.
  • Team admins (Admin, Admin+) additionally see skill ratings, submitted requests and ideas, invitations and the team audit log.
  • Skill ratings are visible to regular players only if the team admins turn that option on (it is off by default).
  • Profile pictures and logos are served from a hard-to-guess web address; anyone who has the exact address can open it.
  • The controller (Iordan Robert-Adrian PFA) and people it authorises may access data strictly for support, security and legal compliance.

5. Data about other people

Admins can add guest players (people without a BIBS account) to lists with a name, a rating and a position, and can invite people by email. When you do so, please add only what is needed (e.g. a first name) and let the person know. An invited person receives a single invitation email and can ask for their data to be deleted by writing to privacy@bibs.app.

9. Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: More → Your profile → Account → Delete your account. You can also ask by email. Full details, including what is kept and why, are on the Account deletion page.

In short, on deletion: your name becomes “Former player”, your email is replaced with an unusable address, your password and Google/Apple links are removed, your picture and nicknames are deleted, you leave every team, your invitations and devices are deleted and you are signed out everywhere. An internal identifier with no identifying data remains so other players' history stays correct. Lists of games already played may still show the display name you had at the time.

Subscriptions: deleting your account does not cancel a subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play. Cancel it in the store settings.

10. Your rights

Under Art. 15-22 GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your data and get a copy;
  • rectification - you can correct much of it directly in the app (name, nickname, picture, email);
  • erasure (“right to be forgotten”);
  • restriction of processing;
  • data portability - receive the data you provided in a structured, commonly used format (e.g. JSON);
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent (e.g. notification permission, from your phone settings), without affecting earlier processing;
  • not be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing (see section 15).

Write to privacy@bibs.app from the address linked to your account. We reply free of charge within one month (extendable by two more months for complex requests, in which case we will tell you). We may ask for information to confirm your identity.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Romanian supervisory authority, Autoritatea Nationala de Supraveghere a Prelucrarii Datelor cu Caracter Personal (ANSPDCP), B-dul G-ral. Gheorghe Magheru nr. 28-30, Sector 1, Bucharest, www.dataprotection.ro, or with the authority of the country where you live or work.

11. Minimum age

BIBS is intended for people aged 16 or older. We do not knowingly create accounts for anyone under 16 and we do not collect parental consent. If you learn that a child under 16 has created an account, write to us and we will delete it. Admins must not add children under 16 as guest players.

12. Push notifications

The app asks for permission to send notifications; you can refuse or withdraw it at any time in your phone settings. You can also choose which notifications you receive per team (signups open, sides ready, match day, announcements). Notifications are sent through Expo to Apple Push Notification service (iOS) or Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android) and contain short texts about team activity, such as the team name and kick-off time. We do not send advertising notifications.

13. Cookies and trackers

This website uses no cookies, trackers, pixels or analytics. The website's hosting provider may technically record IP addresses and browser details in its own logs for security.

The app uses no cookies, advertising identifiers or tracking SDKs and does not track you across other apps or websites. On your phone we store locally only what is strictly necessary: authentication tokens (in the system's secure storage - Keychain / Keystore) and preferences such as theme, language and the selected team.

14. Security

  • All communication between the app and the server is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes; emailed tokens and refresh tokens are stored only as hashes.
  • Access tokens are short-lived; reusing an old refresh token automatically ends the session.
  • Data access is scoped by team and role; request rate limits protect against attacks.
  • Image metadata (including GPS location) is stripped on upload.
  • You can review and sign out signed-in devices from your Profile.

No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach poses a risk, we will notify ANSPDCP and, where the risk is high, you, in line with Art. 33-34 GDPR.

15. Automated decisions

The app automatically splits players into sides using skill ratings and can toss a coin automatically. These operations organise a friendly game, can be adjusted by admins and have no legal or similarly significant effect on you; we do not carry out profiling within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.
