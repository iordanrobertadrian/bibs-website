9. Reporting, blocking and moderation

We do not tolerate abusive content or behaviour. In BIBS:

  • Reporting: you can report a member (from the squad list), an idea or the team name and logo (from More) right in the app, choosing a reason; the report reaches the team admins and the BIBS team. You can report anything else from More → Report abuse or a problem, or by writing to support@getbibs.app with the team name and a description (who, which content, when). You can also tell the team admins.
  • Blocking a user: you can block any member of your teams; you no longer see their ideas, rating requests, nickname and picture, and their actions no longer send you notifications. They are not told. You can unblock them at any time from your profile.
  • Removal from a team: admins can remove a member at any time, revoke invitations and change or reset the join code; a removed member can no longer see the team. You can leave a team at any time from the app.
  • Moderation: we review reports and act on content that breaches these terms within 24 hours: we remove the content and, where appropriate, suspend or block the account that posted it. We tell you about the decision and the reasons, and you can appeal by replying to our email.
