Football with your mates, without the group-chat chaos.

BIBS runs your amateur football group's weekly games: who's in, who's a reserve, balanced sides, score and history. All in one app.

Coming soon to theApp Store Coming soon toGoogle Play

Currently a free beta.

Signup lists

The list opens by itself every week. First come gets in, the rest are reserves, and free spots fill automatically.

Balanced sides

Admins set skill ratings and BIBS splits players into sides as even as possible. No arguments at the pitch.

Match day

Coin toss, match clock, score and rotation of the sides waiting on the sideline.

History and stats

Who came, who won, how many games everyone played.

Notifications

Know when signups open, when sides are ready and when it's match day.

Private, no ads

Invitation-only groups, no ads, no trackers. Delete your account any time.
