How it works

From “who's in?” to kick-off.

No lists pasted in the group chat, no maths on paper, no arguments over picking sides.

Create your group

Pick the day, the time and how many players. Share an invite link or code and the team is set.

Players sign up

The list opens by itself every week. First come, first in; the rest wait, and free spots are filled automatically.

BIBS picks the sides

Balanced sides based on everyone's level, plus a shared clock and full history. You just play.

Features

Everything a football group needs.

Built for the weekly game with friends, colleagues or neighbours.

Sign-ups

The list runs itself.

Sign-ups open automatically on the day and at the time you choose. Everyone joins with one tap, and once the list is full the rest go on the waiting list.

  • Opens by itself every week
  • A freed spot goes to the next one waiting
  • Notified when the list opens and when you get in
Sides

Balanced sides, no arguments.

BIBS splits players into sides that are as even as possible, by rating, position and who has played together before. Everyone sees their side and which shirt to bring.

  • Two, three or more sides
  • Laid out on a pitch, by position
  • A coin toss decides who kicks off
Match day

One clock, on every phone.

An admin starts the clock and everyone sees the same time, live. It stops by itself when time is up, even if nobody is looking.

  • You choose how long a game lasts
  • Pause and resume with one tap
  • Next game with one tap
Squad and history

Know who turns up.

Your squad with everyone's position, rating and games played. History shows every week and who comes most often.

  • Attendance over recent games
  • Search and sort the squad
  • Rating reviews, voted on by admins

Useful notifications

List open, spot freed, sides ready. Everyone picks what they get.

Questions

In short.

Does every player have to pay?

No. The subscription is per team and one person pays for it, usually the organiser. Everyone else uses the app for free.

Does it work for any day and any format?

Yes. Pick the day, the time, how many players and how many sides. You can run several groups, each with its own schedule.

How are balanced sides made?

Admins give each player a rating, and BIBS splits players so the sides are as close in strength as possible. Players can ask for their rating to be reviewed.

What happens to my data?

We don't sell data and we use no ads or trackers. You can delete your account from the app at any time. Details in the Privacy Policy.
